Guide

Get the most out of Collab Digest

Collab Digest shows every Production board collab where you're the CFS, sorted by what needs you first. No more scrolling the board or opening items one by one. It reads Monday.com live with your own Monday.com token after you sign in with your company Google account, and refreshes every 5 minutes. The only thing it changes in Monday is an update you post yourself from Post update.

Try the page

Example data

This is a working copy of the real page with made-up collabs. Click a group chip, then click a row to open it.

New since your last visit

A green dot marks collabs with a new post or stage move since you last opened the page. The New since … chip lists just those.

✓Mark handled

Open a row and press Mark handled once you've dealt with it. It stays hidden until the collab moves to a new stage or someone @mentions you, then comes back with a "Back:" tag saying why. Team chatter and your own posts don't bring it back. Your marks are saved in this browser only.

✉Draft message to creator

Opens a ready-to-edit Brkaway message based on the collab's stage, with an optional Rewrite with Claude. Copy it and paste into Brkaway.

≡By client view

Switch By urgency to By client to see collabs grouped by brand, handy when one client's batch slips together.

Which group would it land in?

Each collab sits in exactly one group: the first that applies, in chip order. Test yourself on a few real-world cases.

04

What the page leaves out

Finished and handed-off work is hidden so the list stays short. Flip the switch to see what would be hidden in the example above, and why.

If a collab you expect is missing, check its stage in Monday first. It may also list someone else as CFS.
