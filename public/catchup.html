How it's sorted. Each collab gets points for its deadline (late and due soon score most), for what the message says (urgent wording, a request or a question), for how long nobody has answered, and for the person who tagged you following up. Finished or on-hold collabs score lower. Act now is 65+, Today is 35+. A tag counts as handled once you posted on that collab afterwards or you mark it done.
What it reads. Updates and replies on your collabs, plus recent updates across your Monday account. Monday doesn't let apps read its notification inbox, so tags it can't see in an update or reply won't show here. Nothing is changed in Monday.com.
